KYLO ABC CAR

Drive, race and learn the ABCs.

A driving game for young children: race around the world, smash things, open treasure chests and learn every letter of the alphabet along the way. Letter games only appear when you buy parts, paint or cars, never while driving.

Support

Questions or problems? Please open an issue at github.com/willlululuplayground/kyloabc/issues.

Parent area

Press and hold the gear button on the main screen for three seconds to open the parent area: steering mode, daily play time, the child's name and letter progress.

Privacy

The app collects no data, has no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases. Read the full privacy policy.
